eConsent · required

One quick consent and you're in.

To send you disclosures, loan estimates, and closing documents electronically — instead of by paper mail — we need your consent under the federal ESIGN Act. You can withdraw any time and switch back to paper.

ESIGN Act § 7001 — full disclosure

Your consent to electronic records and signatures (ESIGN Act)

By checking the consent box, you agree to receive all mortgage-related documents — including Loan Estimates (LE), Closing Disclosures (CD), privacy notices, adverse action notices, and any other communications we are required to deliver — in electronic form rather than on paper.

What you'll need to view the documents

A device with internet access, a modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge from the last 24 months), and a valid email address. PDFs require any PDF reader. If you can't view a particular document, contact your loan officer and we'll provide an alternative.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time at no cost by emailing your loan officer or replying to any disclosure email. After withdrawal, we'll deliver future disclosures on paper. Withdrawing consent will not invalidate any electronic record or signature you've already made.

Updating your contact information

You're responsible for keeping your email address current with us. If your email changes, sign in to your borrower portal and update it — or contact your loan officer directly.

Requesting paper copies

You may request a paper copy of any document at any time at no charge. Email your loan officer with the document name and we'll mail it within five business days.

Confirming consent

Checking the consent box on this page constitutes your affirmative consent under ESIGN Act § 7001. The date, time, IP address, and device information of your consent will be recorded and retained as part of your loan file.
