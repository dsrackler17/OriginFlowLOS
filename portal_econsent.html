Electronic Communication Consent

You are about to apply for a mortgage loan with your lender through OriginFlow. Federal law requires that we provide certain disclosures and documents to you in writing. With your consent, we will deliver these documents electronically rather than on paper.

  1. What you are consenting to. You are consenting to receive the following electronically: your Loan Estimate, Closing Disclosure, initial and annual privacy notices, Adverse Action notices (if applicable), Notice of Right to Receive a Copy of the Appraisal, and any other federal or state disclosures relating to your loan application and closing.
  2. Your right to receive paper copies. You may request a paper copy of any disclosure at no charge by contacting your loan officer. Requesting a paper copy will not be treated as a withdrawal of your consent to receive other documents electronically.
  3. Your right to withdraw consent. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting your loan officer. If you withdraw consent, we will deliver all subsequent disclosures on paper. Withdrawal will not affect the legal effectiveness of disclosures already delivered electronically.
  4. How to update your contact information. If your email address changes, you must notify us immediately. You can update your email address in your borrower portal under Account Settings, or by contacting your loan officer.
  5. Hardware and software you need. To receive electronic disclosures, you need: a device with internet access; a current web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge — last two major versions); the ability to view and save PDF files; an active email address; and sufficient storage to retain documents you choose to save.
