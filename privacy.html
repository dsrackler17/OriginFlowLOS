Privacy Policy

Effective: May 5, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 5, 2026  ·  Version: 1.0
This Privacy Policy explains how Rackler Software LLC, doing business as OriginFlow ("OriginFlow", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit our website or use the OriginFlow LOS service. Mortgage data is sensitive. We try to handle it the way we'd want our own handled.

01Who we are & what this covers

OriginFlow LOS is a software-as-a-service loan origination system used by licensed mortgage branches, lenders, and brokers ("Customers") to originate, process, underwrite, and close residential mortgage loans. This Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our marketing site (originflowlos.com and related properties).
  • Authorized Users of the Service — loan officers, processors, underwriters, closers, branch admins, and viewers acting on behalf of a Customer.
  • Personal information of Borrowers that we process on behalf of Customers as part of providing the Service.

This Policy does not govern how a Customer (the licensed lender) collects or uses Borrower information for its own loan origination, credit, or compliance purposes. For that, you'll want to read the Customer's own privacy notice and GLBA notice.

02How we act: controller vs. processor

Whether OriginFlow is a "controller" or a "processor" / "service provider" depends on whose data we're talking about:

  • For website visitors and Authorized Users: We act as the controller (or "business" under CCPA/CPRA) of personal information we collect directly — for example, the email and name you give us when you sign up, billing details for paying customers, and analytics about your use of the marketing site.
  • For Borrower information processed inside the Service: We act as a processor (or "service provider") on behalf of our Customer. The Customer (the licensed lender or branch) is the controller of that data. We process it only on the Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with our Terms and any data processing addendum we sign with the Customer.

05Borrower financial data & GLBA

GLBA · Safeguards Rule We treat Borrower financial information as nonpublic personal information ("NPI") under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.

When we process Borrower NPI on behalf of a Customer, we act as the Customer's service provider. We:

  • Use NPI only to provide the Service and only on the Customer's instructions.
  • Do not disclose NPI to third parties except (a) to subprocessors needed to provide the Service (see Section 07), (b) as the Customer directs, (c) as required by law or compelled by valid legal process, or (d) to protect the rights, property, or safety of OriginFlow, our Customers, Borrowers, or the public.
  • Maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the security, confidentiality, and integrity of NPI, as required by the GLBA Safeguards Rule. See Section 09.
  • Require subprocessors that handle NPI to be bound by confidentiality and security obligations consistent with our own.

The Customer (the licensed lender) is responsible for providing its own GLBA privacy notice to its Borrowers. We do not provide a privacy notice to Borrowers on the Customer's behalf.

06How we share information

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Subprocessors and service providers who help us deliver the Service (hosting, AI extraction, payments, network security, email). They are bound by contract to protect the information and use it only for the services they provide to us. See Section 07.
  • Within a Customer's branch, where Authorized Users of the same branch can see loans, borrowers, and documents scoped to that branch by row-level security at the database layer.
  • At the Customer's direction, for example when the Customer integrates the Service with a credit bureau, AUS, title vendor, or other third party.

  • In a corporate transaction: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality obligations and to Customer's existing rights under the Terms.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA. We do not rent, trade, or barter personal information.

09Security

Security is the table-stakes feature of an LOS. We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These include:

  • Encryption at rest. The Supabase Postgres database and storage buckets that hold Customer Data and Borrower documents are encrypted at rest by the underlying cloud provider.
  • Encryption in transit. All connections to the Service use HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Row-level security (RLS). Every query is scoped to the user's branch at the database layer. Even a leaked anonymous API key cannot read another branch's data.
  • SSN minimization. Plaintext SSNs are not stored. We keep a salted hash and the last four digits, with full SSN encrypted and minimized only where temporarily required for a credit pull.
  • Field-level audit logging. Every insert, update, and delete on compliance-relevant tables is captured by database triggers, including the actor, role, and timestamp, in a non-bypassable audit log.
  • Malware scanning. Files uploaded to the document library are quarantined and scanned before being made available to Authorized Users. Files flagged as malicious are not processed.
  • Password policy. Minimum 8 characters at signup, hashed and salted by our auth provider; we never see your plaintext password.
  • Access controls. Production access is limited to the founder; access is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you without undue delay as required by applicable law and our agreement with you.

10Data retention

TRID-aligned default We retain loan-related records for a minimum of 25 months by default — matching TRID recordkeeping requirements.

Specific defaults:

  • Loan files, borrower records, conditions, and documents: retained for at least 25 months after the action giving rise to the record (consistent with 12 C.F.R. § 1026.25 (TRID) and § 1002.12 (ECOA)). Customer may configure longer retention (for example, 36 months for ECOA/Reg B records, or longer where state law applies).
  • Audit logs: retained for the life of the underlying record plus the retention window above.
  • Account data for Authorized Users: retained while the account is active and for a reasonable period after closure to address billing, support, or legal matters.
  • Server and security logs: retained for up to 12 months.
  • Marketing-site analytics: retained according to Google Analytics defaults.
  • Backups: may persist for a limited rolling period after deletion before being securely overwritten.

On account cancellation, see Section 11 of our Terms for the data export window. We may retain records longer where required by law, regulation, or to enforce our agreements.

11Your rights (CCPA / CPRA — California)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you, including categories, sources, purposes, and the categories of third parties we've shared it with.
  • Access a copy of the specific pieces of personal information we have about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions (including ones that apply to a lender's recordkeeping obligations).
  • Opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. As stated above, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information. We use sensitive personal information only as needed to provide the Service.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To exercise these rights, email dsrackler@gmail.com with the subject "California privacy request." We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can also designate an authorized agent. We aim to respond within 45 days.

If your request involves Borrower information processed on behalf of a Customer, we will refer the request to the Customer (the controller) and assist them in responding.
