Credit Report Authorization

FCRA §604(a)(2) requires your written authorization before we can pull your credit. Read carefully, then check the box and submit.

By checking the box below, you authorize your lender and its agents to obtain a consumer credit report on you from one or more consumer reporting agencies for the purpose of evaluating your application for a mortgage loan.

This authorization covers:

  • An initial credit report at application
  • Updated credit reports as needed during loan processing and underwriting
  • A refresh credit report shortly before closing

A credit inquiry resulting from this authorization is generally classified as a hard inquiry, which may have a small, temporary effect on your credit score.

You also authorize your lender to share your credit report and related information with investors, servicers, mortgage insurers, and other parties who have a legitimate business need in connection with your loan.

Co-borrower notice: Each borrower must provide separate authorization. If you have a co-borrower or co-applicant, they will receive their own authorization request.

By submitting, you also confirm that the information you've provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge. Knowingly providing false information on a mortgage application is a federal crime under 18 U.S.C. §1014.
