Step 1 of 8

About you.

Let's start with how you appear on your driver's license, passport, or other official ID. We'll use this exact spelling on every document.

You must be at least 18 years old to apply for a mortgage.
We'll collect your full SSN later, encrypted, only when needed for credit pull.
Affects how property is titled in some states. We don't share this with anyone outside your lender.
Step 2 of 8

Where you live now.

Your current home address. If you've been there less than two years, we'll ask for your previous address on a later step.

Step 3 of 8

Your job & income.

Your primary employment. Use the income you can document with paystubs or tax returns — we'll verify everything when the application is complete.

If less than 2 years, we'll ask for prior employment on a later step.

Monthly income (gross)

Before taxes. If your income varies, use the average over the last 24 months.

You're not required to disclose alimony, child support, or separate maintenance unless you want it counted as income.
Step 4 of 8

Your savings & debts.

A high-level picture of what you have and what you owe. Approximate is fine here — we'll get exact numbers from statements later.

Assets

Estimated balances. You don't need exact numbers right now.

Stocks, brokerage, gift letters, etc.

Monthly debt payments

Recurring monthly minimums. We'll pull exact figures from your credit report later.
