How it fits

You don't rip out your system of record.
You stop living in it all day.

Your system of record — Encompass, or whatever your shop runs — is genuinely good at the one job it exists for: investor delivery, the official file, the heavy compliance machinery a big shop needs. The problem was never that it exists. The problem is that you have to work in it all day. OriginFlow is the fast workspace you actually work in. Spotlight · The PDF drop zone

Drop a paystub. Watch the condition clear itself.

This is the feature that lands every demo, and it's live on real files today. A processor — or you, on an iPad with a borrower at the kitchen table — drops a PDF onto the loan. Claude Vision reads it, classifies the doc type, pulls the structured numbers, and writes them back to the loan record. The matching condition auto-clears. No re-keying, no stare-and-compare.

The audit log records the AI confidence score and model version, so a compliance officer can replay the extraction months later and check the math. Low-confidence reads route to a manual review queue instead of silently passing. You stay safe and you stay fast.

Honest status: paystub extraction and auto-clear are running in production now. W-2 and bank-statement extractors are next on the bench — same pipeline, more doc types.

Who built this

A submarine, a spreadsheet, and a frustrated loan officer.

Before OriginFlow, I served on the USS Montana during dry dock in Newport News, Virginia. What you learn in that environment is that everything runs on procedure, verification, and trusting your instruments when your instincts are screaming something else. That discipline shows up in every line of code in this product.

After separating from the Navy, I built Submarine Catalyst, a biotech research tool with a paying customer base and a published track record — including the misses. Same submarine aesthetic, same obsession with making the machine do the boring work. It proved I could ship production software that customers pay for every month.

OriginFlow started because a loan officer friend in Texas wouldn't stop complaining — not about which system his shop used, but about how slow it was to work in all day. 60-second file loads, a shared Excel pipeline tracker, no real collaboration, an admin headcount his branch couldn't afford. His shop needs its system of record — it's wired to investors and compliance. But every one of his actual complaints was about speed, and every one was a thing I could fix without touching those relationships.

So I built the fast front end that sits in front of it. Schema first, then Claude Vision into the document pipeline so the file reads its own paystubs, then the live pipeline board and the conditions engine. And — going deeper than a front-end tool has any reason to — the underwriting decision and the closing workspace, because he kept finding more of his day I could give back. He's the first tester and the reason every feature exists. If it doesn't fix something a working LO has actually complained about, it doesn't make the cut.

The goal was never to sink the battleship. It's to be the fast boat the loan officer actually wants to drive all day — and let the heavy ship do the one job it was built for.

A processor (or you, on an iPad with a borrower in front of you) drops a PDF onto the loan file. The doc gets stored and an extraction record is created. A serverless function sends it to Claude Vision with a prompt scoped to the doc type, parses the response into structured fields, and writes them back to the loan record. A matching condition auto-clears. The AI's confidence score and model version are recorded in the audit log; anything under a configurable threshold routes to manual review. Paystub is live today; W-2 and bank statements run on the same pipeline and are next up.
Row-level security at the Postgres layer scopes every query to the user's branch — even a leaked anon key can't see another branch's data. A hash-chained, tamper-evident audit log records changes on compliance-relevant tables with actor, role, and timestamp. SSN is never stored in plaintext. And because your system of record doesn't change, the heaviest compliance and investor-delivery machinery stays where your shop has already validated it — OriginFlow adds a fast workspace in front, it doesn't ask you to re-certify your whole compliance stack on day one.
